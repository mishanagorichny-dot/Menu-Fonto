Forno was born from a simple belief: that the oven is the heart of the table. We bring together Europe's oldest cooking traditions — wood fire, slow roasting, stone-baked dough — and treat them with the same care they've always deserved. Every plate that leaves our kitchen carries that philosophy: honest ingredients, unhurried technique, and food made to be shared. At Forno, we don't chase trends — we tend the fire, and let it do the talking.
